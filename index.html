Quality Assurance & Testing Protocol

Every harness gets 100% electrical continuity & crimp pull testing, polarity checks and IPC/WHMA-A-620 workmanship review before it leaves the bench.

1

100% Electrical Continuity Testing

100% end-to-end electrical continuity: every single wire and pin is verified on modular test boards for zero open circuits or cross-wiring.

2

Sacrificial Crimp Pull-Force Testing

Batch-level crimp pull testing to guarantee terminals never slip under heavy vibration.

3

Pinout & Polarity Verification

Double-checked against your schematic/BOM before dispatch to protect sensitive flight controllers and servers.

4

IPC/WHMA-A-620 Workmanship Standards

Strict adherence to strip-length accuracy, zero nicked copper strands, and proper bend-radius protection.

Common Questions

Do you really accept single-unit (1 piece) prototype orders?

Yes. Nityacon has zero minimum order quantity (MOQ). We build single-piece prototypes and small batches for startups, MSMEs, R&D labs and college teams, and a rough hand-drawn pinout sketch is enough to get a quote.

Can you source the wires and original JST/Molex connectors from our BOM?

Yes. We offer turnkey BOM and component sourcing: wire, terminals and original JST and Molex connectors as listed in your BOM. You can also supply your own parts. Lead times for sourced parts are confirmed in your quote.

Do you ship custom wire harnesses across India?

Yes. We offer on-time pan-India delivery. Every harness is continuity tested and dispatched ready to plug in, with the delivery timeline confirmed in your quote.
